How to read these numbers

Accuracy Out of 100 cases, how many the model got right.

Cases found (recall) Of all people who truly have the condition, how many the model caught. Low here means missed cases.

Confidence Shown on your report. It is how sure the model feels, not a guarantee it is right. Below 70% treat the result as unclear.

These results come from public research datasets, not hospital trials. Real scans vary by machine, quality and patient, so real-world accuracy can be lower. Use a result to decide whether to see a doctor, never to decide that you don’t need one.

Know the limits

Built to inform, not to diagnose.

A model can help you ask better questions. Only a doctor, with your full history and the original images, can tell you what you have.

  • Models can be wrong, especially on images unlike the ones they were trained on.
  • A low confidence score means the result is uncertain, so treat it that way.
  • A "no abnormality" result does not rule out disease.
  • For severe symptoms or an emergency, contact a doctor or emergency services right away.
